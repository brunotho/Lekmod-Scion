Civilization No Quitters




Civ 5 Full Rule Set
  

General Game Rules
* No leaving a game, unless:
   * You have lost your capital city, 
   * All players agree your civ is irrelevant to the development of the game, 
   * All players agree the game is over,
   * Real life issue comes up (frequent users of this rule will be monitored).
* Only play when you have enough time, six man games usually last more than 6-7 hours.
* Any use of exploits will get you banned.
* If a player disconnects from chat all players must wait at least 10 minutes for them to return. If the player does not return a majority vote should be taken to determine whether the game should be continued. The exception is if a player does not return, and the game is at turn 30 or earlier. In this case a unanimous vote is required to continue.
* If the game crashes, ALWAYS meet in chat for a reload. 
* If a game is experiencing technical problems (e.g. 3 or more resyncs within less than 10 turns, players dropping repeatedly or multiple crashes) the game can be scrapped for technical reasons. Players should ALWAYS try to fix these problems first (resetting router, restarting steam or switching to a different host if one is available). A single crash or resync is NOT a sufficient reason for a technical scrap.
* If all players wish to restart the game, they can do that with a unanimous remake vote. All players from the original game should be given spots in the new game. This privilege doesn't apply if the game was scrapped.
* Everyone is required to promptly respond to scrap, cc or irr votes. The preferred way to conduct votes is through the in-game Lekmod interface. You’re not allowed to repeatedly start the same vote, this includes in particular the situation in which the vote done via in-game interface has not been concluded. If there’s a good reason for it (for instance you don’t want to finish a wonder after asking for irr), you may roll the turn to get players to respond to the vote.
* Scrap vote is unanimous. In case all remaining players except one want the game to end, a cc vote to the one objecting player has to be passed.
* Rolling or pausing the turn is only allowed in exceptional circumstances:
   * Technical or real life issues including agreed game pauses.
   * Responding to last second moves or allowing the opponent a response.
   * Voting on scrap, concede or irrelevance.
Diplomacy and World Council
* Trading with other players is allowed - NO Gifting of resources/gold that gives you no logical benefit in the game (e.g. gifting all your gold and offering free lux deals before you die).
* Trading oil to players who haven’t researched Biology is not allowed.
* Peace agreements lasting more than 30 turns are not allowed, this includes agreements not to use specific timing or type of unit (e.g. no frigate pact, if it extends beyond 30 turns horizon).
* Non-aggression pacts are not allowed after any player reaches the Information Era.
* All diplomatic interactions with AI except declaring war or making white peace is forbidden. No abusing AI during war to level up units. No using Open Borders to move units through AI territory, even if the open borders deal was established before they became an AI. No diplomats in AI capitals for extra votes if you are attempting a diplomatic victory or for increased Tourism output.
* No city trading. Three exceptions to this rule: If you are in a war with someone and it is your original city you can take it back in a peace deal. If you are in a war with someone and you wish to return a previous allied city state back to its original ally at the time as part of peace reparations. Tourism, if your city was flipped due to pressure and the pressure caused a city flip, it may be traded back to its owner. 
* A player can only vote for themselves on world leader votes.
Warfare
* Shift-moves are NOT allowed.
* “Scorched Earth” style tactics are not allowed. Selling buildings, replacing tile improvements and starving cities just to grief the attacker is not allowed. No selling buildings before atomic era.
* Throwing civilian units into the ocean to block embarked units is not allowed.
* Repairing tiles outside your territory to pillage them again is not allowed.
* You may not liberate a city if doing so would place that city under AI control. This rule does not apply to city states.
* Abusing city states for training units, pillaging and repairing tiles, and making peace every turn while conquering are not allowed. Worker stealing is allowed.
* Immediately declaring war on a player after allying his city state(s) is not allowed. This includes allying the city state at the end of one turn and declaring war at the beginning of the next turn. The previous owner (that had allied it for a period of time) must always be given at least one full turn timer to react. During this one turn, the previous ally may buy back the city state and declare war; the player attempting to take the CS from the original owner does not get this perk.
* Promotions acquired when the unit was trained cannot be used for healing. Saving promotions by waiting for the turn timer to end is not allowed. Saving promotions by making an attack without picking a promotion first is allowed.
* If you lose control of your capital you need to leave the game after 10 turns unless ANY of the following conditions is true:
   * You are presently able to recapture your capital.
   * You still control the majority of your original cities (this includes only the cities you owned at the start of the war and majority means MORE than half, so e.g. controlling two out of the original four is not enough).
   * You have realistic winning chances.
Those conditions are checked at the end of the 10 turn period and at the end of every turn afterwards unless you regain the capital and keep it for 10 turns at which will reinstate the 10 turn counter if you lose your capital again afterwards. 
* No planting new cities after the start of the war with the purpose of allowing you to stay in the game after your original cities have been captured. The exception to this is if doing so gives you realistic winning chances.
* It is not allowed to abuse non-aggression pacts to position units where they normally couldn’t be positioned in a state of war, build roads on the borders, block settlement spots, use missionaries, prophets or generals, while peace-blocked.
General Code of Conduct
* All players should be respectful and display good sportsmanship; abusive players will be kicked. Do not abuse game mechanics intentionally.
* During Reload, moving before a hotjoin is not allowed.
* Suiciding on purpose can be considered worse than quitting. All players should look long term and play to win and not just ruin it for others.
* Should a player in your game stream DO NOT watch his/her stream! It will result in a warning/ban.
* Collusion or predetermined team play is not allowed. Playing not to win or helping another player to win is not allowed.
* Selling and rebuilding low-hammer buildings (e.g. shrine) to generate production overflow is not allowed.
* Gifting any type of Great Person is not allowed under any circumstances.
* Building Oxford University more than once is not allowed.
* Saving policies, spy steals or free technologies by not ending your turn for more than 1 turn is not allowed.
* Allowing another player to liberate a City State you have captured in order to manipulate world leader vote requirements is not allowed. Capturing or marrying a City State you are allied to on the turn of the world leader vote to lower the vote requirement is not allowed.
* Using interface mods giving access to information that's not available in the base game is not allowed. This includes the standard version of EUI mod, if you want to use EUI mod please refer to the discussion board post on how to make it compliant with this rule.
* Avoid changing your nickname constantly, this is a small community and people gain reputations and shouldn't need to check your previous aliases to avoid you if you are a toxic/connection issues type player. Constant changing may result in a kick if you are attempting to fool players on your identity.
If a clear violation of a rule has occurred a reload should take place, however if all players agree you may continue.
Banned/kicked players cannot use group resources (e.g. group chat), including using other people to form lobbies - if people wish to assist banned players in "proxy" playing in the group further punishment may be applied or punishment towards the player assisting. To prevent ban evasion, banned/kicked players can be banned from group chat until they have been reinvited.
Chat is exclusive for members only, the only exceptions to this are if you are awaiting membership to join the group as a new member or you are a banned player seeking support from the Moderation team about your situation
By using the No Quitters resources (ie. Steam Chat, Voice Communication Server) you agree to all of the rules above.
