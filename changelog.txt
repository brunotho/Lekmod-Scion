LEKMOD CHANGELOG
================
Format (also the source for player patch notes on the website):
- "## <release>" opens a release block; "## UNRELEASED (...)" collects work since the last release.
- Each item: NAME (TYPE_KEY), dashed underline, "Tags: a, b" line, then a short player-facing summary.
- The first tag is the item's group (the website's section header); further tags are filters.
- Player-facing lines: the summary and "- Added:", "- Changed:", "- Removed:", "- Fixed:";
  a reworked system's card says how it works now with "- Now:" lines (plain bullets), plus Removed/Fixed.
  They describe the net change compared to the previous release (for the first block: LEKMOD v35.4),
  in quick-speed numbers, for players with no development knowledge. Bugs we introduced and fixed
  before a release are not player-facing.
- Developer-only lines (skipped for patch notes): "- Note:", "- DLL:", "- Dev:".
- Tags: tourism, world congress, espionage, policies, civs, buildings, units, promotions, religion,
  city-states, diplomacy and trade, tiles and resources, game mechanics, ui, bugfix,
  combat, movement, naval, air, wonders, ruins.
- The tag order is the website's section order; the last six tags are filters only (never a first tag).
- "Weight: minor" (optional line after Tags) lists the item under "Minor changes" at the end of its section.
- "(NEW)" after the name marks an item that did not exist in the previous release: NAME (NEW) (TYPE_KEY).
- The summary line is optional: only a gist the lines don't already say (no restating, no praise).
  A pure fix gets bugfix as its first tag.

- Now: spies placed in a city cost 3 Gold per turn per era from the Renaissance (3 / 6 / 9 / 12 / 15); diplomats are free
- Now: spies and diplomats see around their city with a radius of strength minus visible defense (none at 0 or less), updated every turn

- Now: a spy steals every 15 / 13 / 11 / 9 / 7 turns at strength minus visible defense 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5+ (5 more turns per point below 1); the timer follows changes every turn
- Now: effects that slow down steals add up and lengthen the steal timer: Iron Curtain +50%, Kremlin +30%, Great Firewall +25%, Diplomatic Transparency +50%
- Now: a steal always succeeds and gives 8 turns of the city's Science toward a technology you pick; the victim always learns who stole
- Now: each steal can get the spy caught: 10% per Constabulary, Police Station and Intelligence Agency, plus a part from the guarding spy's level minus the spy's strength (-4 or less 0%, -3 5%, -2 10%, -1 20%, 0 33%, +1 50%, +2 67%, +3 83%, +4 or more 100%; at most 100% in total); the guarding spy is hidden from the attacker and doesn't slow the steals

- Now: level 3+ spies can switch to full-tech steals: a whole technology of your choice instead of Science, it succeeds with the chance not to be caught times a share by the spy's level (level 3 half, level 4 three quarters, level 5+ all of it) and a failure gives nothing; the catch chance stays the same
- Now: a level 3+ spy guarding your own city can sweep it for 5 turns of that city's Science in Gold; every enemy spy there is caught with its usual catch chance
- Now: a spy in a City-State is Meddling: it takes 0.2 per turn per level off every other civilization's Influence change there (above the resting point it falls faster, below it recovers more slowly; they don't learn whose spy it is), and it shows its coup chance
- Now: a diplomat in another civilization's capital gives you 2% per level of that capital's Science (halved at war; only while they know a technology you lack) and +15% per level Gold on your trade routes to that city
