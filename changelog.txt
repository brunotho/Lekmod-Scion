LEKMOD CHANGELOG
================
Format (also the source for player patch notes on the website):
- "## <release>" opens a release block; "## UNRELEASED (...)" collects work since the last release.
- Each item: NAME (TYPE_KEY), dashed underline, "Tags: a, b" line, then a short player-facing summary.
- The first tag is the item's group (the website's section header); further tags are filters.
- Player-facing lines: the summary and "- Added:", "- Changed:", "- Removed:", "- Fixed:".
  They describe the net change compared to the previous release (for the first block: LEKMOD v35.4),
  in quick-speed numbers, for players with no development knowledge. Bugs we introduced and fixed
  before a release are not player-facing.
- Developer-only lines (skipped for patch notes): "- Note:", "- DLL:", "- Dev:".
- Tags: promotions, units, buildings, civs, policies, religion, ruins, combat, movement, naval, air,
  game mechanics, world congress, ui, art, bugfix.

WAR ON CITY-STATES (STANDING AND PENALTIES)
-------------------------------------------
Tags: game mechanics, ui
Declaring war on city-states now escalates through four standings, with lasting penalties on Influence.
- Changed: each declaration of war on a city-state counts (also repeat declarations on the same one). Your standing: 1 Antagonist, 2 Provocateur, 3 Aggressor, 4+ Warmonger
- Added: Wary city-states have a lower resting Influence with you: -10, -20 or -30
- Added: Alienated city-states lose Influence with you faster: +50% or +100% decay
- Added: Antagonist: the city-state you declared war on becomes Wary (-10) and Alienated (+50%)
- Added: Provocateur: every city-state you declared war on becomes Wary (-30) and Alienated (+100%); the two other city-states closest to your capital that you have met become Wary (-20)
- Added: Aggressor: as Provocateur, and all other city-states become Wary (-20) and Alienated (+50%)
- Added: Warmonger: all city-states become Wary (-30) and Alienated (+100%)
- Added: city-states allied with you when you declare war are not affected; penalties never decrease
- Added: Nemesis: a city-state you declare war on 3 times never makes peace with you
- Removed: the old Aggressor/Warmonger rules (double Influence decay after 2 declarations, random city-states becoming wary)
- Added: the city-state screen shows Nemesis instead of the peace button, the status tooltip lists Wary/Alienated and your standing, and the war button tooltip shows what declaring war would do. A notification tells you your new standing
- Dev: LEKMOD_CS_WAR_DECLARATION_PENALTIES: CvMinorCivAI::DoTeamDeclaredWarOnMe (levels from CvTeam::GetNumMinorCivsAttacked), anchor in GetFriendshipAnchorWithMajor, decay multiplier in GetFriendshipChangePerTurnTimes100, nemesis in IsPeaceBlocked. Lua: GetMinorCivWaryLevel, GetMinorCivAlienatedLevel, GetMinorCivWarDeclarations, IsMinorCivNemesis (on the city-state), GetCityStateWarDeclarations (on the major)
- Note: city-state personalities with BlocksWarDeclarationPenalty (none at the moment) still skip all of this
